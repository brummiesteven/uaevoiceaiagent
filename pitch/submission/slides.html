03 · Why live web data

The answers aren’t in any API.

Tariffs and entitlements exist only as rendered pages — and Dubai’s firewall rejects datacentre traffic outright. Without context.dev routing through a UAE address, a server cannot reach them at all.

It works. It’s slow — 15 to 25 seconds — so today it runs just ahead of the call.

04 · What the agent does on its own

Nothing orchestrates it.

It hears the question, picks one of 10 tools, chooses the arguments, reads the result, and decides whether that answered it.

One rule above all others: never state a number that didn’t come from a tool. Ask what it doesn’t know, and it says so.

06 · Hardest problem

A bug that told us nothing.

Multi-word searches returned zero results. No error, valid JSON, an empty list that looked exactly like “no matches”. The same URL through curl returned 22.

Node’s built-in fetch and the portal disagreed, and neither reported it. Found by testing one URL through three clients and comparing.
